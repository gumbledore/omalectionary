Westminster Leningrad Codex (WLC) -- Public Domain
=====================================================

Used in data/wlc.json, built by build/convert_wlc.py from
https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb (wlc/*.xml).

The Westminster Leningrad Codex text itself is public domain. Only the WLC
consonantal/pointed Hebrew text (the <w> word content of each <verse>) is
used by this plugin; the morphhb project's morphological tagging, parsing
data, and VerseMap.xml (used at build time only, to normalize WLC
versification to KJV/English numbering -- see convert_wlc.py's header
comment) are licensed CC BY 4.0 by the Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible project
and are not shipped in data/wlc.json.

https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb (About.md / LICENSE.md):
